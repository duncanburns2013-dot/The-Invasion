Breaking News

Latest developments in Massachusetts immigration policy

PROTECT Act Signed Into Law

August 5, 2026

Governor Maura Healey signed the PROTECT Act at La Colaborativa in Chelsea, calling it "the strongest law in the country to protect people from ICE."

What the law does:

  • Blocks ICE from making warrantless arrests at schools, hospitals, courthouses, and childcare centers
  • Bans new 287(g) agreements that deputize local police as federal immigration agents
  • Creates civil rights claims against government officials, including ICE agents, who violate constitutional rights
  • Requires judicial warrants for immigration arrests in protected locations
"The PROTECT Act is the strongest law in the country to protect people from ICE, because no one should be afraid to send their child to school, seek medical care or fear reporting a crime." — Governor Maura Healey

Federal Response: The Department of Homeland Security opposed the measure, warning that Massachusetts lawmakers were "releasing violent criminals from jails" and that federal officers would need "a more visible presence" to find them.

Also today: Healey condemned ICE's plan to rent 250 parking spots in downtown Boston: "We absolutely cannot allow ICE to have 250 parking spots in downtown Boston while they are engaging in these illegal tactics."

Shelter Spending Data Confirmed

November 6, 2025

WBUR obtained official state spending data through public records request after the Healey administration stopped publishing biweekly reports:

  • FY25: $978 million spent on emergency shelter and related services
  • FY24: $894 million spent
  • Combined: $1.87 billion over two fiscal years
  • Per family rate: $1,182 per week as of October 2025

Since July 2025, the administration has spent $109 million, with $56 million on shelter locations and $50 million on exit programs like HomeBASE.

Policy Changes Enacted

February 2025

Legislature passed $425M supplemental with major reforms:

  • 6-month stay limit for families in shelter
  • 4,000 family cap for calendar year 2026 (down from 6,012 in Jan 2025)
  • Massachusetts residency requirement for shelter access
  • Competitive bidding required for all EA program expenditures
