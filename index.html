How Massachusetts Compares

State and local costs from illegal immigration (CBO, FAIR 2023). Massachusetts is one of four states bearing the heaviest shelter burden.

Key finding: The Congressional Budget Office confirmed that state and local governments in New York, Massachusetts, Illinois, and Colorado spent $3.3 billion combined on shelter services in 2023 alone—accounting for 90% of the national increase in asylum seekers in shelters. Meanwhile, the federal government collects payroll taxes while states pay for schools, shelters, and healthcare.

The Cost Shift

The Congressional Budget Office found the immigration surge reduced federal deficits by $0.9 trillion over 10 years—because immigrants pay payroll taxes for Social Security they may never collect. But state and local governments bore a net cost of $9.8 billion in 2023 alone for education, shelter, and healthcare. The feds profit while states bleed.
